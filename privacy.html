Privacy policy

Block Puzzle 2 · Effective 27 October 2026 · This is a plain-language policy for a small game. It is not legal advice.

The short version

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts, and there are no accounts. The game keeps almost everything on your own device. A random player id and the username you pick are sent to our server only so the leaderboards work.

What stays on your device

  • Your games in progress, best scores, stats, missions, badges, coins and power-ups.
  • Your settings (theme, sound, block style and so on) and a random player id.
  • This is stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing the site data in your browser removes it all.

What we send to our server

  • Leaderboards: your random player id, the username you chose, the board (daily or weekly, size and mode) and your score. Usernames and scores are public on the leaderboard.
  • Reporting a name: if you report a leaderboard name, we receive your player id and the name you reported.
  • Technical data: like any website, our host sees your IP address and browser details when you load the game. We use a scrambled (hashed) version of the IP address for a short time to stop abuse such as spam requests.

How long we keep it

  • Daily leaderboard scores are deleted after about 3 days and weekly scores after about 12 days.
  • A username stays reserved to its player id until it is changed or removed.
  • Reports about names are kept for moderation (the latest 500).
  • Rate-limit counters last from a minute to a day.

Who handles the data

  • Vercel hosts the game and runs its small server functions.
  • Upstash provides the database for leaderboards.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface, so your browser contacts Google to load it.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Ads

The “ads” in this version are placeholders and collect nothing. If real rewarded ads are added later, the ad provider may use advertising identifiers under its own policy, and we will update this page and ask for any consent the law requires first.

Children

The game is for a general audience and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us a username that identifies them, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser's site data to delete everything on your device and get a new player id.
  • To have a username or leaderboard entry removed from our server, contact us with the username.

Contact

Contact email: to be added before the app is released.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Using the game after that means you accept the update.
