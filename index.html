    How to play

    1. Place. Drag a piece onto the board, or tap a piece and then tap where it goes.
    2. Clear. Fill a whole row or column to clear it.
    3. Combo. Clear lines on back-to-back turns for bonus points.
    4. Daily. Everyone gets the same pieces. One go a day, and an ad earns another.

    The game ends when none of your pieces fit. Easy shows your next 3 pieces, Medium shows 1, Hard shows none.
