    How to play

    1. Pick. Choose a level, a board size (6\u00d76 to 12\u00d712) and Classic or Crazy mode at the top. Crazy mixes in wild shapes sized to the board.
    2. Place. Drag a piece onto the board, or tap a piece and then tap where it goes.
    3. Clear. Fill a whole row or column to clear it.
    4. Combo. Clear lines on back-to-back turns for bonus points.
    5. Daily. Everyone gets the same pieces. One go a day, and an ad earns another (up to 3 extra). Play on consecutive days for a streak.
    6. Bombs. In Crazy, some pieces hide a bomb. It shows once the piece is placed, and when its row or column clears it blows up the blocks around it. Bigger boards get bigger blasts.
    7. Hammer. In Crazy there is a hammer too: tap it, then tap any block to smash it. One free per game, then an ad earns another.
    8. Help. Undo takes back your last move and Hint shows a good spot. Each has one free use per game, then an ad earns another.

    The game ends when none of your pieces fit. Easy shows your next 3 pieces, Medium shows 1, Hard shows none. Timed gives you 2 minutes. Puzzle sets a goal with a fixed board and pieces.
